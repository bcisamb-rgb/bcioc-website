California's SB 440 Has Changed the Rules.
Are You Protected?

For the first time in California history, private construction owners face the same strict legal claims process that public agencies have dealt with for years — with penalties that can be swift, severe, and financially devastating.

Contractors — especially large, well-organized GCs — already know this law inside and out. They have legal teams, claim specialists, and scheduling experts ready to file the moment a dispute arises. Most private owners do not. That imbalance is exactly what SB 440 was designed to exploit — and it puts unprepared developers at serious financial risk.
